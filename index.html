Pocket Scorecard

The scorecard for pitch & putt and golf. No account, no ads, pay once.

Try the app (tester preview)

On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: Chrome menu, then Install app. Your scores are saved on your own phone only.

  • One tap per hole: score, putts, tee shot, penalties
  • Pitch & putt and golf, 18 or 9 holes
  • Add your course from a photo of its scorecard
  • "Tip me" when the round goes sideways
  • Range plans built from your own rounds
  • Works offline; your data never leaves your phone

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email pocketscorecardapp@gmail.com and include your phone model.

Moving to a new phone: in the app, go to Data → Share backup, save it, then use Restore on the new phone.
