Privacy Policy

Pocket Scorecard · Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version

Pocket Scorecard does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Your rounds, scores, courses, scorecard photos and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. We cannot see your data and have no copy of it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No location access.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a course from a photo of its scorecard, the app asks to use your camera or photo library. The photo is shrunk and saved on your phone only, so you can check the card later. You can remove it at any time.

Exports and backups

When you export a CSV or a backup, the file goes only where you choose to send or save it using your phone's share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete rounds and courses inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
